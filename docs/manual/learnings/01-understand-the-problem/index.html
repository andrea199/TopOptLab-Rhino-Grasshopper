One consistent problem

120 × 60 mm plate in World XY; thickness 2 mm; left edge fixed in X and Y; force (0,−100,0)N at (120,30,0)mm. These are the new starter’s dimensions, rather than the smaller benchmark shipped with the plugin.

End-of-block checkpoint: each student can identify the design area, constrained edge and load direction, and make a qualitative deformation prediction. Grid creation starts in the next block.
